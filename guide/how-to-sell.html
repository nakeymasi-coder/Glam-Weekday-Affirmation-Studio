How To Sell Your Designs

Ten ways to turn your finished posters into income. Open one, try it, tick it off.

1. Turn your images into PNG products

Export your images as high-quality PNG files with clean backgrounds. Sell single PNGs, bundle several into packs, or build themed collections such as faith, boss energy and glow-up.

2. Sell prompt packs

Instead of selling only the images, sell the prompts you used to create them. Include image, video and music prompts together. Sell on your own store or on Etsy.

3. Sell physical products with print-on-demand

Turn your images into real products with no inventory. Use Printify to put designs on t-shirts, hoodies and mugs, and sell wall art prints and posters.

4. Use mockups so buyers can picture it

Use Rapid Mockup to create product previews. Show your design on shirts, frames and posters, and use those images in your listings.

5. Create digital wall art

Offer printable wall art in several sizes (8x10, 11x14, 16x20) and bundle prints into collections. Sell on your own store and on Etsy.

6. Make phone wallpapers and digital packs

Create lock screen wallpapers and aesthetic bundles. Package matching designs together so a buyer gets a full set.

7. Sell Canva templates

Upload your images to Canva, build editable text designs around them, and sell them as templates.

8. Create sticker packs

Turn images into PNG stickers and sell digital sticker packs for planners, iPads and Cricut.

9. Let content bring the traffic

Post videos using your images on TikTok, add a clear hook and caption, and link your product in your bio. Show the finished result first, then how you made it.

10. Choose where to sell

Your own store gives you the most control. Etsy is great for discovery. Gumroad is simple for digital downloads. Shopify is more advanced. Start with one and do it well.

Final tip. Do not just create. Package your work, present it well, and sell it with confidence.
