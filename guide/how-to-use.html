How To Use The Studio

Nine short steps. Tick each one as you go.

0 of 9 steps done

1. Build your image prompt

Open the Studio and tap a preset, or choose your own categories (style, lighting, scene and more). Add custom text if you want something the lists do not have. Tap Generate, then Copy Main Prompt and paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Meta AI or your image tool.

Tip

On a phone the result scrolls into view for you, and a small message confirms when you copy.

2. Understand lighting

Soft lighting is clean and natural. Golden hour is warm and glowing. Studio lighting is sharp and high-end. Neon lighting is bold and trendy. Low light is moody and dramatic.

Tip

Pick lighting that matches the feeling of the affirmation.

3. Understand camera and angles

Eye level feels natural and relatable. Low angle feels powerful. High angle feels soft and lifestyle. Wide shot shows the full body and the whole scene. Close-up focuses on the face, use it only when you want it.

Tip

The Studio keeps the full body in frame unless you choose otherwise.

4. Use the video direction

After you generate your prompt, the Studio writes an 8-second video direction. Use it in your video tool for TikTok, Reels or Shorts, and follow the movement notes. Add your own voice or a caption with the affirmation text if you like.

Tip

Generate your poster image first, then use it as the identity reference for the video.

5. Make music

Pick genres, a style description and a vocal type, adjust the sliders for the vibe, then generate. The jingle, 30-second and 1-minute boxes each give a different structure. Paste your favorite into your music tool.

Tip

Style choices are sound descriptions, such as warm neo-soul or bouncy amapiano-pop groove.

6. Use the marketing content

Get captions, hashtags and promo copy for the day you chose. Pick the one for the platform you are posting on, copy it, and post.

Tip

Hashtags follow your chosen weekday.

7. Save and reuse

Tap Save Prompt to keep a prompt on your device. Restore brings back the prompt and the settings you used. Delete removes one, Clear History removes them all.

Tip

Saved prompts live on this device only.

8. Build your own helper (optional)

Copy the assistant instructions into the custom GPT builder in ChatGPT to make your own helper for content or products.

Tip

Skip this until you are comfortable with the basics.

9. If you get stuck

Ask ChatGPT or Gemini for help. Try: Give me luxury lighting ideas. Or: Give me outfit ideas for a glam character. Or: Give me scene ideas for a rooftop shoot.

Tip

Start simple. Generate. Adjust. Repeat.
