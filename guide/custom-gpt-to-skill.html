Turn your Custom GPT into a Skill

Plain words, one step at a time. About 10 minutes.

What is confirmed

Custom GPTs retire on December 11, 2026. After that date the GPT and its page are no longer reachable. OpenAI's replacement is plugins, and a plugin holds one or more skills. Your GPT can keep working until then.

OpenAI says its "Migrate to plugin" button (in My GPTs) turns your GPT's instructions into a skill and copies your knowledge files in as reference files. Your chat history, sharing settings and chosen model do not come along, and a migrated plugin starts private.

Heads up: OpenAI's own help page blocked my automated check, so these details come from news reports that quote it. Open the official page below and compare before you rely on it. Migration can also arrive on different dates for different accounts.
  • OpenAI: Custom GPT retirement and migration FAQ (official)
  • Campus Technology report (Oct 5, 2026)
  • Claude Help: Using Skills in Claude (official)

Path 1: Use OpenAI's Migrate button

Best if your GPT already lives in ChatGPT. Per OpenAI, the latest published version is what moves.

Path 2: Make it a Claude Skill

Works even if you never had a Custom GPT. Steps 5 to 7 are from Claude's official help article. The file format in step 3 follows the common Skill layout, so also read Claude's "How to create custom skills" article before you rely on it.

Builder: your outputs become the skill files

Instructions become the skill. Knowledge becomes the reference file. Starter Questions become example requests.
